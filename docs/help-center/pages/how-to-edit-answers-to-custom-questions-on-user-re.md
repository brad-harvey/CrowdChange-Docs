# How to Edit Answers to Custom Questions on user request (Multi-Registration sites only)

**N.B.**This feature only applies for transactions made to fundraisers with our multi-registration flow.

If a donor or registrant makes a mistake while answering your custom checkout questions, or simply changes their mind, don't worry! You have the ability as an Admin user to make the adjustments in CrowdChange.

From the Admin Registration Report, each registration listed will have a **Change** button in the rightmost column, which you can click on to adjust answers to custom checkout questions. You may also adjust registrant **name** ('username' in Admin Reports),**display name** (in public Donation Lists), edit any displayed **comment** and set whether to show or hide the transaction **amount**.

Follow these simple steps to change answers to custom questions:

1. Go to your CrowdChange site and **Log In**.

2. Click **Profile** in in the top right corner, then**Admin**.

3. Go to **Reports** **\> Registration Report**using the left sidebar.

4. Click the **Change**button next to a registration.

   ![299307.png](../assets/how-to-edit-answers-to-custom-questions-on-user-re/299307.png)
5. Scroll down to the custom checkout questions (last in the list) and make your desired adjustments.

6. Click **Save**to confirm your changes.

   ![edit2.png](../assets/how-to-edit-answers-to-custom-questions-on-user-re/edit2.png)
