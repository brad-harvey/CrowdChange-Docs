# Editing or Deleting Activity Records

Participants have the ability to **Edit** or **Delete**any records previously entered in the Activity Tracker.

This functionality is available for all metric types (Days, Hours and Distance).

Follow the simple steps below in order to to edit or delete a record.

## To Edit a Record

1. Go to your **Personal Page** and scroll down to the**Activity Tracker** section.

2. Click **Manage Activity**.

3. Under **My Records** click **See more records**.

4. Click the **triple-dot icon** next to the record you want to edit and click **Edit**.

5. Make the necessary changes and click **Save**.

## To Delete a Record

1. Go to your **Personal Page** and scroll down to the**Activity Tracker** section.

2. Click **Manage Activity**.

3. Under **My Records** click **See more records**.

4. Click the **triple-dot icon** next to the record you want to delete and click **Delete**.

5. Click **Yes**.
