# Did not receive a confirmation email / receipt

You should receive an automatic email confirmation after making a donation and/or purchasing a ticket (with the PDF ticket attached).

If you have not, please try the following:

1. Check your email **spam folder** to ensure the email wasn't accidentally filtered out.

2. Log In to your **Profile** to ensure you entered the correct email address. See [How to Access Your Profile](how-to-access-your-profile.md)

3. In your Profile, go to the **My Receipts** tab for a log of all your transactions and tickets. If your transaction went through, it will be recorded here. You can **Download Tickets** and **Get Receipts** using the respective buttons next to each transaction.

If you think you may have made a **typo** in your email address, reach out to us via the blue **Help**button in the bottom right corner of this page, and we'll be happy to assist!
