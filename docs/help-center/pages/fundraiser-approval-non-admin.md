# Fundraiser Approval (Non-Admin)

CrowdChange is strictly an online fundraising platform, and therefore we don't have authority to approve or reject fundraisers on your site.

It is only your **Charity /** **Organization** who has the authority to do so.

We recommend reaching out to them directly to ask about the approval status of your fundraiser.
