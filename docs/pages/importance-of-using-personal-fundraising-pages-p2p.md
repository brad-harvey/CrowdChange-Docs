# Importance of using Personal Fundraising Pages (P2P)

Personal Fundraising Pages are CrowdChange's **most effective tool** for raising more money with your fundraisers!

We recommend that everyone in your Chapter creates a Personal Fundraising Page and shares it with five people outside of campus. We've seen a ton of success with this method and Chapters have raised over 235% more than they would have originally.

It takes only **30 seconds** for each brother or sister to create their own Personal Fundraising Page!

For step by step instructions, see **How to Create a Personal Fundraising Page \& Share It**
