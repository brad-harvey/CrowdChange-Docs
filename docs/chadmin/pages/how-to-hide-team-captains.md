# How to Hide Team Captains

For events where Teams are created by your organization rather than participants, there is no need to indicate Team Captains. In these cases, the Team Captain names and icons/identifiers can be hidden.

Follow the simple steps below to disable Team Captain icons on your fundraiser:

1. Go to your CrowdChange site, **Log In**, and navigate to your fundraiser.

2. Click **Manage Fundraiser** in the top right corner of the page.

3. Click **Peer to Peer settings** on the left sidebar.

4. Enable '**Hide team captain**' under 'Main Features'.

   ![image-20240819-170409.png](../assets/how-to-hide-team-captains/image-20240819-170409.png)

Once enabled, team captain names and icons/identifiers will be hidden from all public pages.
