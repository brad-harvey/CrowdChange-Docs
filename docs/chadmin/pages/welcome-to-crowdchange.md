# Welcome to CrowdChange!

Thank you for choosing CrowdChange as your fundraising solution!

Our mission at CrowdChange is simple; to partner with our clients to provide an intuitive, comprehensive fundraising platform so they can focus on what matters most; their cause. We see our team as an extension of your organization and aim to help your fundraising efforts reach new heights.

The **Help Center** will be your main portal to everything CrowdChange - from how to create your first fundraiser in just a few minutes, to more in-depth questions about tax receipts, peer to peer fundraising, and back-end reporting - the Help Center will be a resource for you to explore and share with your fundraising team.

Here are a few helpful resources to get you started:

* You can create a custom fundraising page on CrowdChange in under five minutes! [Click here for more information on **How to Start a Fundraiser**](how-to-start-a-fundraiser.md)

* Looking to set up **Tax Receipts** to be generated automatically at the point of donation? Take a look at our guide to [**Tax Receipts \& Eligibility**](tax-receipts-eligibility.md)**.**

* The **Site-Level Access Manager** allows you to assign administrative roles to your users, allowing them full or partial administrative privileges. [Click here for more information on the **Site-Level Access Manager**](how-to-use-the-site-level-access-manager.md).

* Want more information about back-end reporting?

  * The [**Admin Donations Report**](how-to-access-the-admin-donations-report.md) will provide you with a high-level overview of all transactions on your CrowdChange site.

  * Is your fundraiser set up for **Registrations** ? The [**Registrations Report**](https://chadmin.crowdchange.help/ct/How-to-Access-the-Registrations-Report.2022834323.html)will give you a detailed overview of all registrations made or purchased through your fundraiser.

  * Does your site use the **Recurring Donations** function? For all information plus the ability to skip, change the date, or cancel a recurring donation, take a look at the [**Recurring Donations Report**](https://chadmin.crowdchange.help/ct/How-to-Access-the-Recurring-Donations-Report.2037448799.html)

  * Does your organization have **Tax Receipts** enabled? The [**Tax Receipts Report**](how-to-re-issue-a-tax-receipt-tax-receipts-report.md) shows any receipts issued and allows you to cancel or reissue a tax receipt.

Additionally, the CrowdChange team is only ever one call away. For one-on-one support, you can always reach out to your organization-specific Partner Success representative and we would be happy to jump on a call with you. For any support-related inquiries that require immediate assistance, you can also email [support@crowdchange.co](mailto:support@crowdchange.co) and someone will get back to you within 24 hours.

Best of luck on your fundraising journey!
