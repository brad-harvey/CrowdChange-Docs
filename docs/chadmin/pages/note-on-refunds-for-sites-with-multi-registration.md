# Note on Refunds for Sites with Multi-Registration

This section applies only to sites with our **Multi-Registration** flow enabled.

## Issuing Refunds

During the regular [**Refund**](how-to-issue-full-and-partial-refunds.md)process, you can also refund (multi-)registration transactions, including self-donations, made during the registration process. In order to do so, follow these steps:

1. Go to the **Admin Donations Report**(Profile \> Admin \> Reports \> Donations Report), and select the correct transaction.

2. Click the **Refund** button in the bottom right of the menu that appears.

3. Select the specific items you want to refund, check amounts and enter a reason for the refund.

4. Your chosen items will be refunded. This may affect items created or used during the initial transaction (teams, pages, tickets, etc).

**Regarding P2P Pages created from Registration:**

When you refund a **Registration** (even a free one), it will delete the fundraising page made during the registration process. This will **not** refund the donation, made during multi-registration.

If you want to refund the donation made during the multi-registration process, you must select the correct entry under the "**Self Donations**" block.

Refunding only the donation, made during multi-registration, will **not** delete the attached fundraising page and will **not** refund the registration ticket price.

So, in order to fully refund everything a person paid for a single registration during the multi-registration process, you must refund both the "**Registration** " and "**Self Donations**" blocks.
