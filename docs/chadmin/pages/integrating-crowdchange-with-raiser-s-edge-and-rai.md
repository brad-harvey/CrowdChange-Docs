# Integrating CrowdChange with Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT

**Importacular** by Zeidman Development is your import tool for bringing all CrowdChange information into Blackbaud Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT. See the video below for a brief introduction to Importacular:

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2gSMEB9gJXk>  
CrowdChange charges a one-time fee to build a custom API in order to connect your CrowdChange site to Importacular. This fee also covers the development costs of maintaining this connection over the life cycle of the CrowdChange platform.

For details, please contact your Partner Success Manager to discuss.

## **Installing Importacular**

Importacular is a free plug-in for Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT. It is available for both Blackbaud-hosted and self-hosted accounts.

If your Raiser's Edge or Raiser's Edge NXT account is hosted by Blackbaud, Importacular is available as a default plug-in. If you do not see Importacular in your plug-ins section, contact [Zeidman Development](https://www.zeidman.info/contact).

If you are self-hosted, please [register for a **ZeidZone** account](https://www.zeidman.info/registration/) to download the plug-in.

### **Using Importacular**

Once the Importacular plug-in is installed, take a look at our [CrowdChange Import Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rSV5PSzdTu4) video for further information on how to integrate your CrowdChange site with Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT.  
Please note that you will need to determine the mapping of all fields within Importacular.

See the below list of all fields available to map into Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT from CrowdChange using Importacular.

[Importacular - CrowdChange Data Fields.pdf](https://chadmin.crowdchange.help/__attachments/a_a618160dacc4249cba6992875487c27356f366aa052a2dde2405b45024487234/Importacular%2520-%2520CrowdChange%2520Data%2520Fields.pdf?cb=b833e367496b94ef884b1a293a9b6763)

Here are is some more information you may find useful:

* [Tribute/In-Memory Import](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbOSwxQ8VbI&feature=emb_title) / See article [here](https://www.zeidman.info/docs/importacular-user-guide-3/import-to-honor-memorial-and-tribute/) as well.

* [Importing Constituent Data](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxHZb0hXwI8&feature=emb_title)

* [Importing Addresses with Importacular](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ciDMeeljbU&feature=emb_title)

* [Importing Gifts into Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oXNFWXMFf50&feature=emb_title)

* [Creating a mapping template](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl7IzJcV74I&feature=emb_title)

Please note that data flow from CrowdChange to Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT through Importacular is **not**automatic. You must manually initiate the upload in order to see results in Raiser's Edge and Raiser's Edge NXT.

### **Looking for support?**

Your main support portal for the Importacular plug-in will be the [Zeidman Development **Importacular User's Guide**](https://www.zeidman.info/docs/importacular-user-guide-3/overview/). This is a comprehensive online knowledgebase detailing how you can best connect your CrowdChange site with Importacular. This guide contains installation instructions and explains the complete functionality of the Importacular plug-in.

Many troubleshooting solutions are available via a quick search in the [**Zeidman Development Support Page**](https://zeiddev.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/folders/270245), which outlines issues that users have raised to Zeidman Development.

All additional support from Zeidman Development is available at a cost.

**Zeidman Development does not offer real-time support, even to those who do opt-in to their paid support option.**
