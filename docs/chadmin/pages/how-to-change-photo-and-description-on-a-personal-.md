# How to Change Photo and Description on a Personal Page

You can **customize** your Personal Fundraising Page by adding your own **photo** and **description text**, in place of the main fundraiser defaults!

To do so, follow the steps below:

1. From your Personal Fundraising Page, click **Manage my Page** in the top right.

   ![image-20240821-200716.png](../assets/how-to-change-photo-and-description-on-a-personal-/image-20240821-200716.png)
2. Click**Page Settings** and toggle **OFF** these options:

   1. **Use default image and video**

   2. **Use default description**

      ![arrows.png](/__attachments/a_771c7dea93a0a3123fb57536ea44f712701ff14224eeaf176bd4831b7025f4d8/arrows.png?cb=65e508118f64bd32d6cd2ee6624d85e9)
3. Upload your image or video, add any desired text, scroll down and click **Save**.
