# Failed Transaction

Failed transactions can be the result of many factors including, but not limited to, the following:

* **Interrupted internet service**

  * Confirm your internet connection is stable and refresh the page, then retry your transaction.

* **Incorrect Billing Address**

  * Please make sure that your billing address is an exact match to the billing information associated with your Credit Card or else the payment will not be able to process.

* **Time-out**

  * Time-outs are common and usually caused by the following:

    * Page being left open for an extended period of time causing the token to expire.

    * Switching apps while filling out the form / billing info.

* **Internet browser being used**

  * Try a different web browser, or an incognito / private tab. We recommend Google Chrome. If you are still having trouble, turn off all plugins such as Adblock, refresh the page and try again.

* **Mistake in the payment information entered**

  * Double and triple-check that all payment information is correct.

Re-attempt your transaction. If you are still unable to process your transaction please contact us by clicking the blue **Help**button in the bottom right corner of this page.

## CSRF Token Mismatch

This is a common error message which can be caused by the following:

1. **Browser plugins** such as Adblock

2. Using **Incognito / Private browsing modes**

3. **Time-outs** (page being open for an extended period of time causing the token to expire) - Time-outs can also be caused on some devices by switching apps while filling out the form / billing info.
